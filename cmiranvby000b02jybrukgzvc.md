Marina Abramović was cruel to show us how bad people are. Part of me wishes that she had never done that art piece.

I don't know much about it, to be honest. I just heard of the experiment on an Instagram reel sent to me by my older brother an hour ago. He probably thinks I put too much faith in the humanity in people, and needed to face reality. He is right, I do put a lot of faith in humanity, or rather than faith, I'm just holding on to hope because I need hope to keep on living, it's all I really have.

Calling herself an object is what I hated the most. I know it was only for her piece. I appreciate provocative art, but this one was a bit too much for me.

Calling herself an object is just a statement, words, so why did they believe her? They wouldn’t believe her if she called a cup of poison water? Well, in all truth, they didn’t believe her, they experimented to confirm that she was an object by slowly doing worse and worse things.

Was believing she was an object justified? I don’t think so, but maybe at the moment, and for those six hours, she was indeed an object, so the conclusion of their experiment might have been justified in those six hours.

BUT HOW DARE THEY FORGET THAT SHE WASN’T AN OBJECT, not before these six hours started and not after they ended?

HOW DARE THEY FORGET THAT SHE WAS MOVING AND SHE WILL START TO MOVE AGAIN? Were they actually hoping someone would kill her so she remains an object?

They did hope she’d die at the end, they put the loaded gun in her hand, pointed it towards herself, hoping she would kill herself, so they don’t see her move again.

But how dare I use “they”? Would I have acted differently? Would you? Yes, in most likelihood we would have acted differently, and for my relief at the time of the performance, a “we” and a “they” existed, “we” the people who tried to protect her and “they” the people who violated her.

It remains the case even in that reel comments, “we” hoping to find other people who would have protected her, and “they” are the people who think she got what she was asking for, no in fact, both teams think she got what she was asking for, but “we” think it’s bad, and shouldn’t happen, while “they” think it’s normal and was the only possible conclution. “we” hate it, and “they” don’t see anything wrong with it.

So why are “we” so weak and “they” are so strong? Because “they” are acting and “we” are only reacting? Because “they” are showing human nature and “we” aren’t? Because “they” are bad and “we” are good? Because “they” are united and “we” are divided, doubting if some of “we” would act like “they”? Because “they” are right and “we” are wrong? Or because “they” are wrong and “we” are right?

The top comment was a naive “Those who would defend her → like button” This person might have been hoping to convince themself that “we” are more than “they”. Many of the comments follow the same theme, like some would announce they would have protected her, and some put the blame on men dividing “we” even more.

But “they” commented too, “they” blamed her, said she deserved it, and attacked her character, or simply blamed the situation.

You can see that neither “we” nor “they” is taking accountability. “we” are just blaming “they, and “they” are blaming circumstances or reality; neither is providing a solution.

As much as I want to hate “they”, I can’t dismiss all of these people as bad, not if I want to keep my hope. I think “we” need to do our best to educate “they”, I don’t think it’s about having power or peer pressure, I think it’s their individual lack of belief in the moral rules society forced them into, breaking under the existence of power and peer pressure.

“we” should focus on providing “they” with the foundation to build a stronger consciousness, and stop the blame game, and maybe one day, when this art piece is inevitably recreated in the future, we can be proud of how it turns out.
