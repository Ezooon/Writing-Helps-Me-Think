## A Pitch

The idea is simple: Phobonull is a platform for people with different points of view to interact with each other productively, and here, the other side, since most social platforms keep people separated by their points of view, and they don’t interact enough. And Phobonull enables that by letting people of a demographic post their questions and concerns about other demographics and hear responses directly from that other demographic.

That sounds good, but is it too long? Is it clear enough? Did I forget to mention a crucial point? Maybe I should have a smaller version…

Alright, Phobonull is a platform that lets people ask questions directly to the people they disagree with without raising unproductive arguments (maybe I can make a platform for productive debate to…).

Yeah, short and informative, and keeps them curious, but again, please comment any suggestions, I’m looking for actual feedback, engagement is nice, but I need feedback more at the time.
